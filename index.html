What Makes Medical Murals the Best

Advanced Printing

We use the latest technology printer available. Medical Murals UV Printing System costs 1.5 million dollars with ultra-high resolution and special ink blends to produce life-like murals from 100,000 images.

Quality First

We print directly onto acrylic, not Duratrans film. Our products won’t fade, bubble, or peel. We use Healthcare Grade Acrylic with proper fire safety ratings, ensuring durability and safety for medical environments.

Safety

Medical Murals use genuine Bayer® Acrylic Sheet with outstanding optical properties and light disbursement. Our Healthcare Grade Acrylic provides 1.20"/Min Flammability Rating and 820°F ignition temperature for maximum safety.

Health-Care Innovation

All Medical Murals contain light-activated broad-spectrum antimicrobial coating that resists mold, mildew, fungi, algae and odor-causing bacteria. Proven healthcare-grade protection at no additional cost.
