Frames, Domes & LED Light Panels

From custom architectural soffits and breathtaking sky domes to energy-efficient LED light panels, Medical Murals crafts immersive ceiling environments that transform any space into a place of calm and wonder.

Custom Architectural

Frames & Soffits

Our frames and soffits are manufactured using high-density architectural foam with a hard coat finish. They are very lightweight, strong, and easily attached to any ceiling — making installation simple and cost-effective without sacrificing quality.

We can produce these in any size, shape, color, or texture to match your exact specifications. For example, the horseshoe soffit can be made 8' x 10' with a smooth finish and color matched to the wall paint — creating a seamlessly integrated look that feels custom-built.

  • High-density architectural foam construction
  • Hard coat finish for durability
  • Extremely lightweight yet structurally strong
  • Any size, shape, color, or texture available
  • Color matching to existing wall paint
  • Easy ceiling attachment — no heavy rigging required
