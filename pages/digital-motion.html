Wall & Ceiling

Video Walls — The Ultimate Attention Grabber

This new technology provides video loops to use as an artificial window or skylight. We can also divide videos across multiple monitors or TVs for a seamless panoramic experience. Look out your false window and watch the waves flow in and the trees sway.

Listen to the sound of a calm beach setting, or watch the clouds move past as you gaze up at the ceiling. These wall and ceiling videos can be mounted horizontal or vertical, and we offer a variety of ceiling frame and window/shutter packages.

We have a large assortment of video loops to choose from, or we can customize one to your taste — including content, speed, and length.

Virtual Marine Life

Virtual Aquariums

Our aquariums are displayed on an LCD monitor or gang of monitors that we frame into a wall anywhere in your facility. The real-time movement is fascinating to watch and requires absolutely no tank maintenance.

These systems are computer-based, so the fish and backgrounds can be easily changed to suit the season, theme, or preference of your patients — from tropical coral reefs to tranquil freshwater scenes. A captivating focal point that keeps patients calm and distracted.

Star Fields & Cove Lighting

Fiber Optic Displays

We use fiber optic technology to create breathtaking Star Fields, elegant Cove Lighting, and fully custom lighting displays. These installations create an otherworldly atmosphere that patients find deeply calming and mesmerizing.

Fiber optic panels can install as ceiling tile or into any type of surface — walls, soffits, curved ceilings — giving you complete creative freedom. Perfect for MRI rooms, waiting areas, children's wards, and anywhere a sense of wonder is needed.
