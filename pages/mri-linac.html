Specialized Solutions for Medical Imaging Rooms

Medical Murals provides expertly designed murals and lighting systems tailored for MRI, CT, and Linear Accelerator environments. From non-ferrous filtered solutions to custom-built frameworks, we have the experience to meet even the most demanding installation requirements.

Non-Ferrous Systems

MRI Rooms

MRI Rooms require non-ferrous filtered lighting systems for Virtual Skylights and Windows. Medical Murals carries a large selection of LED light boxes for walls and ceilings including slimline wall hanging systems (see Plug & Play).

Our newest product line includes a one-piece 4' x 6' mural and LED light box system, combining premium aesthetics with proven medical-grade engineering to create a calming environment for patients undergoing imaging procedures.

Non-Ferrous Materials LED Light Boxes Slimline Systems 4' x 6' Murals
Patient Comfort

CT Scan Rooms

Good CT Scans require a cooperative patient. Our backlit ceiling murals distract and calm the patient during imaging procedures, significantly improving image quality and patient comfort.

The back lighting is available in Superbright, Standard, Lowlite, and Dimmable options. The acrylic murals can be ordered as High Gloss, No-Glare, or in a Mat finish, giving you complete flexibility to match your facility's specifications and aesthetic needs.

Patient Calming Superbright LED Dimmable Options Multiple Finishes
Custom Engineering

Linear Accelerator Rooms

Linear Accelerator Rooms typically have very crowded ceilings with laser alignment systems above the gantry. These challenging environments demand specialized solutions that Medical Murals has extensive experience delivering.

We design custom frames or soffits that complement the shape of the accelerator system while accommodating the laser alignment system. We also offer drop-down systems to fit under obstacles, ensuring a seamless integration with your existing equipment while providing the calming visual benefits your patients deserve.
