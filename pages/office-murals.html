Three Easy Installation Options

Choose the method that works best for your existing fixtures and space — all are simple, fast, and tool-free.

1

Replace the Lens or Diffuser

We cut the mural to replace the lens or diffuser in your existing or new fluorescent light fixture — up to 4' x 4'. Drop it right in and enjoy instant transformation with zero hardware required.

2

Velcro Frame Cover

We provide the mural with a Velcro-type strip around the edge that adheres over the outer frame of your fixture. Covers both the frame and lens for a clean, seamless finish — and removes easily whenever you want.

3

Stand-Off Mural

Our stand-off mural is spaced away from the wall or ceiling to create a distinctive floating effect. The gap between the panel and surface adds depth and dimension — a truly contemporary design statement.
